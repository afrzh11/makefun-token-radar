How the ranking is worked out

implied = TVL × peer multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier

  • Peer multiple. The median market-cap/TVL of the token-bearing protocols in the same category, when at least 3 peers exist; otherwise the median over every token-bearing protocol (this snapshot: —). Clamped to 0.05–2.50, so one frothy category cannot set the board. A protocol is worth what its category’s traded peers are worth per dollar of TVL.
  • Growth multiplier. 1 + (7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100%) ÷ 100 × 0.5. A protocol growing fast is worth more than one shrinking at the same size.
  • Maturity multiplier. 0.70 + min(age in days, 1095) ÷ 1095 × 0.45. Older protocols are more likely to be real and to launch; unknown age counts as 365 days.
  • Coverage. The board covers all 40 watchlist slugs, then every other protocol that passes the same exclusion rule the watchlist uses — no bridge, liquid-staking or exchange product, no versioned pool, no exchange’s name, no wrapped/staked word — whatever its TVL. The floor is where the reading started, not where it stopped.
  • What the numbers are not. The snapshot records no ranking, no implied value and no airdrop flag. A high implied value means “big and credible enough that a launch at its category’s usual multiple would be large” — not that a token is coming, and not what it would list at.
